The design mix is an approximate absolute-volume calculation (IS 10262 style). Assumed: specific gravity of cement 3.15, sand 2.65, stone 2.70; 2% entrapped air; sand is 40% of total aggregate by volume; water 165 L/m³ (160 for M35 and M40), assuming the admixture reduces water. Conversion to cft uses 1,600 kg/m³ for sand and 1,500 kg/m³ for stone (loose), which changes with real density and moisture. A larger 10 mm share makes pumping easier but needs a little more cement. The ready-mix plant's own tested design mix is the final word. When ordering, state the grade, slump, maximum stone size and admixture.

Assumed: dry volume is 1.54 times the wet volume, 1 bag of cement (50 kg) = 1.25 cft, 1 m³ = 35.315 cft. Water comes from the water:cement ratio (M5: 0.65, M10: 0.60, M15: 0.55, M20: 0.50, M25: 0.45, M30+: 0.40). Adjust water for the moisture in sand and stone. Grades M30 and above are made with a design mix and have no fixed standard ratio; the 1 : 0.75 : 1.5 shown for M30+ is only a rough figure, so follow the project engineer's design mix when you have it. For multi-storey buildings or special structures, always use the engineer's design. Results are approximate.
