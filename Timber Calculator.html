Separate calculators for a door, a window, a table and shuttering (formwork). Enter the sizes and get the timber in cubic feet, with a full cut list.

How the timber is measured

Timber volume (cft) = length (ft) × width (in) × thickness (in) ÷ 144. 1 cft = 0.0283 m³. Sizes are finished sizes. Rough-sawn timber is usually sold a little oversize, so use the sizes you will actually buy. A joint allowance of 1 inch per end is added to rails, aprons and heads. Wastage is added on top of the net cut list for sawing loss. All results are estimates, so check them against your own drawing before you order.
